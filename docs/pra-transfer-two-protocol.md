# Two fresh manuscript development comparisons

Run two unused experimental biology papers selected before generation: fly sleep and ageing (10.1371/journal.pone.0308652) and yeast growth polarity (10.1371/journal.pbio.3002491). Both are published versions. This experiment does not estimate overlap with the original human reviewers or test an untouched confirmatory set. The papers become development cases after evaluation.

Use the current candidate-retention instructions and v3 debate handoff unchanged for both papers. Each paper receives one ordinary single review, one structured investigator review, one debate, and one anonymous comparison of single, investigator before debate, and after debate. Both initial conditions may produce eight candidates and select five questions. All conditions share the same source packet, images, model, comment allowance, and question-only format. PRA has two generation calls against one for the baseline. This is not a compute-matched superiority test.

The investigator checks reported results, endpoint scope, and design scope. Debate receives every candidate and discarded hypothesis. It must preserve, resolve, or defer every component. No new deterministic target inventory, evidence-ID schema, or literature integration is introduced during this test. Source failures are measured with the existing validators.

All calls use subscription-backed Sol at medium effort. Eight calls maximum, no retries or model fallback, with a 500,000 observed-token launch stop across both papers. The final in-flight call can cross that stop. Calls run as separate resumable stages with durable receipts. No paid credits or reset redemption. No additional calls may be used to obtain a better result.

Source delivery includes all main figure pages and small supplementary figure text. Sleep also receives both supplementary ANOVA pages and the first replicate figure. Yeast also receives supplementary figures 1 and 5 and both methods tables as text. Other figure pixels, videos, raw data, author code, and external literature are explicitly unavailable. Review histories are excluded. A source omission is not evidence of an absent experiment.

Randomize condition labels separately before inference. The judge sees only selected question text and common source evidence, without workflow identities, hypotheses, decisions, or validation status. Judge each review against the source and choose a preference, allowing ties. Invalid individual evidence references remain unscored. A failed internal debate audit blocks production acceptance. If final questions themselves pass the source and format contract, the planned judge may still compare them as an explicitly exploratory output. Raw outputs and failures remain unchanged. This fallback is specified before inference.

After judging both papers, audit newly found and lost scientific requests, narrowed scope, false premises, disproportionate demands, source limitations, and quoted-evidence failures. Mechanical component preservation does not prove semantic retention. One same-family automated judge per paper and limited unblinded source checks cannot establish scientific ground truth or general superiority. Do not tune between the two papers. Any later fix is unbenchmarked until tested separately.
