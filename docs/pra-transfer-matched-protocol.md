# Two-paper matched-pass transfer protocol

This small prospective development test evaluates the frozen review workflow on two manuscripts absent from the local evaluation corpus. It follows the ecology development comparison and is not confirmatory validation.

## Selected sources

- Clinical trial: DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0321635, a melatonin sleep trial in women with endometriosis. Include the main paper, small study protocol, CONSORT checklist and flow figure.
- Plant physiology: DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0332803, a soybean drought-stress experiment. Include the main paper and the first three figure captions' source pages. Omit the raw XLSX dataset.

Sources were selected as the earliest eligible 2025 original research results from the public PLOS title queries randomized AND sleep and drought AND growth. Protocols, corrections and computational studies were excluded. DOI, title and local paper identity are checked against the prior corpus before generation. This establishes local freshness only.

The clinical checklist was a legacy Word file labeled DOCX by the publisher. Preserve its original bytes and import a converted DOCX copy. Both conditions see the same conversion. Publisher review histories and author responses are excluded. No complete analysis reruns or large raw datasets are used.

## Frozen change and conditions

Add a shared conditional check of the manipulated contrast, accompanying changes and causal attribution. Do not treat every mixture substitution as confounding. The new guidance asks whether the stated claim matches the actual intervention, composition contrast or bundled treatment.

Both conditions use Terra at medium effort, two generation calls, the same sources, initial figures, domain guidance, advisory checks, final five-finding maximum and approximately 900-word comment budget. Both receive four tool calls in their first stage and two in their second stage, with six shared external requests. Actual token counts and tool use are reported separately.

The comparison is two-pass ordinary self-revision versus investigator plus structured debate. The single reviewer receives its own guarded first draft. PRA uses its investigator's candidate inventory and explicit component adjudication. Both apply source-handle validation and edit guards. This compares the workflows as a whole, rather than isolating one prompt sentence.

Freeze code, guidance, manuscript workspaces, images, finite DOI-record packets and this protocol before the first model call. Do not tune prompts between the two papers. Preserve failures and raw outputs. No retries or substitutions are permitted within this run.

## Assessment

A fresh-context Terra judge compares randomized anonymous final reviews against the source manuscript and union of accessible source images. The judge uses immutable evidence handles. Its ratings assess source support, specificity, materiality, proportionality and edit fidelity. Invalid source references remain unscored. Overall preference is secondary.

Report for each condition: delivery success, supported consequential findings, unsupported allegations, excessive requests and observed generation tokens. Separately inspect shared and distinct issues, PRA candidate retention, narrowed or resolved components, and information lost between stages. Coordinator semantic checks are explicitly nonblind and do not count as independent expert validation.

A two-paper result cannot establish a reliable win rate or isolate the contribution of the new checklist. A tie or baseline preference is retained. Failure to deliver counts as a failure, even if a raw draft looks promising.

## Budget

At most ten model calls: four review-generation calls and one judge per paper. The launch threshold is 850,000 observed input and output tokens, checked between stages. Each review condition also has its existing 250,000-token launch threshold. These thresholds cannot cap tokens inside a running call. Unknown usage stops subsequent inference. No paid credits or reset redemptions are authorized.

The larger validation study is preparation only. Its manuscript sample, expert assessment materials, success criteria, analysis plan and projected budget will be drafted from this test. No larger run or expert outreach is performed in this task.
