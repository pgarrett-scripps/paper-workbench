# Targeted investigation and tool uptake

The previous owl comparison configured 13 callable manuscript functions but recorded no calls from either independent reviewer or the debate. Provider adapters and domain guidance are separate from callable functions. Availability alone did not produce investigation.

## Changes

- Independent reviewers must investigate one or two consequential questions with at least one evidence tool within the existing four-call allowance. They may confirm the authors or reject a suspected concern. An irrelevant external lookup is not required.
- Structured tool checks link a scientific question and reported interpretation to the actual tool name and call receipt. Missing, fabricated, ambiguous, failed or note-only receipts do not complete the policy audit. Incomplete investigations retain their scientific content and receive an explicit incomplete audit. There are no automatic retries.
- MCP initialization and tool listing have a separate discovery log. A configured tool list alone is not proof of server exposure or actual use.
- The ecology route now recognizes ordinary ecology terms including natal dispersal, breeding success and habitat selection. Reference-only matches remain excluded. Routing is lexical and can still miss domains or select irrelevant guidance.

This policy applies to independent reviewer generation, including the reviewers used by the ensemble. It does not change the tool-free baseline, ordinary self-revision or debate prompts. Existing frozen runs remain unchanged.

## Bounded development check

One new Terra medium independent reviewer call uses the saved owl manuscript, small supplements and original figure image. No previous reviews, human review history or judge outputs are supplied. The manuscript was already known to the coordinator. This is a development check of exposure and uptake, not a blind comparative quality study or evidence of superiority.

The run is saved under `runs/pra-tool-uptake-v1`. The existing four-call investigation allowance and six-request external limit remain in place. No judge call, full panel, paid credits, reset or automatic retry is authorized by this runner.

Offline validation passed 43 tests covering targeted-check audits, tool discovery and receipts, domain sources, evidence handles and ensemble behavior. Scientific usefulness still requires examining the returned evidence and review.

## Observed result

The single reviewer call completed. MCP initialization and tools/list receipts confirm exposure of all 13 functions. The ecology package was selected through natal dispersal in manuscript block b0001.

The reviewer made one successful inspect_results call for b0037. It traced Figure 1 to the S1, S2 and S3 captions and reported that the check narrowed a concern to causal interpretation rather than a broken result reference. Its final review included the post-treatment parental-departure adjustment question. The targeted-check audit passed with a matching receipt.

This tool returned organized manuscript evidence that was already present in the input. It did not retrieve new external evidence, inspect additional pixels or prove that the review improved. The four final findings were not independently judged in this check. The result demonstrates uptake and traceability only.

The one model session reported 245,314 input plus output tokens, compared with 79,267 for the saved previous independent reviewer. Tool interactions process the large context again, so even one call can materially increase recorded usage. These are reported token counts, not a bill or a count of uncached tokens. No further trial was launched.

The next refinement should distinguish newly acquired evidence, useful reorganization of supplied evidence and redundant checks. Prefer narrow evidence packets for subsequent targeted investigation rather than repeatedly carrying the complete manuscript. Any such context change needs its own bounded validation before claims about cost or scientific quality.
