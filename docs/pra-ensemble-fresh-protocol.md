# Fresh ensemble comparison protocol

Frozen before any model generation. Two development papers compare independent discovery plus debate with ordinary sequential revision. This is a feasibility comparison and does not authorize the planned 24-paper confirmatory study.

## Manuscripts and source scope

- Clinical: *Efficacy of online mindfulness for the treatment of insomnia in pregnancy*, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0322931.
- Plant: *Seed priming and abiotic stress tolerance in carrot*, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0318753.

Both were selected by domain and availability, then checked against 37 locally inventoried prior papers before review generation. Selection is a convenience sample, not random sampling. A previously reviewed soybean paper was excluded during selection. The future 24-paper sample remains design-only. These two development papers will be excluded from that sample through the local corpus inventory and an explicit exclusion record.

Provide the same immutable published manuscript, available small supplements, and initial figure pages to both conditions. No human peer-review histories, author replies, raw participant data, or complete computational reruns are supplied. These are published papers, not initial submitted manuscripts.

The clinical packet includes the latest attached January 2020 IRB protocol and TIDieR checklist. Two earlier long protocol versions are archived outside reviewer inputs. A separately mentioned CONSORT checklist returned HTTP 404 at its supplementary DOI and is recorded as unavailable. The plant packet includes two supplementary figures and the published supplementary ANOVA and post-hoc table, extracted as original cell values with coordinates and no recalculation.

The total clinical input is 28,845 extracted words including the full latest protocol. The input guard is increased from 25,000 to 35,000 words to retain that protocol. The plant packet is 14,725 words. Main article text alone is approximately 7,600 and 8,300 words respectively.

## Conditions and budgets

Use Terra at medium reasoning throughout. Each condition has three logical generation calls and a tool allowance of 4, 4, and 2. Corresponding external request ceilings are 6, 6, and 2. These are permission ceilings, not required use. Actual tokens and tool usage are recorded.

1. Ensemble: independent reviewer one, independent reviewer two, then one fresh debate over their union.
2. Sequential: first review, ordinary self-revision, then a final ordinary self-revision with both earlier drafts available.

Share the first discovery draft between conditions, counting its cost in each logical condition but once in actual study usage. The second independent review cannot see its sibling's findings or tool state. Each initial or intermediate discovery output allows ten candidates. Both final primary reviews allow five findings and 900 comment words. Both conditions retain secondary comments. The sequential final schema permits ten secondary findings, while the ensemble retains all source candidates through its ledger. Report this inventory asymmetry and judge the primary reports separately.

Debate retains its explicit criticism, author defense, evidence, and component destinations. A new brief instruction asks it to examine central claims for consequential gaps appropriate to the design. No category quotas or mandatory allegations are introduced. The ordinary revision instruction already asks for important omissions and corrections.

At most 12 new model calls across two papers: five unique generation calls and one judge call per paper. Stop launching calls at 1,500,000 observed tokens or on unknown usage. The threshold cannot cap a call already running. Use subscription authentication with freshly verified zero paid credits. No automatic model retries, fallback models, or usage reset. Preserve and report failed deliveries.

## Evaluation

One fresh-context anonymous Terra judge per paper receives the same source evidence, both primary reports, and an opaque bank of initial, intermediate, and secondary candidates. It does not receive stage identities, workflow labels, prior judges, or human reviews. Primary labels are counterbalanced: ensemble A for clinical, ensemble B for plant.

The judge assesses every distinct emitted finding for support, specificity, consequence, proportionality, and edit fidelity. Unsupported-allegation judgments must quote the criticized comment exactly and cite source counterevidence. Semantic issue groups must account for every finding exactly once, grouping the same scientific question and resolution rather than general topics.

Assess primary-report quality and preference separately from secondary quality and candidate retention. Track supported issues added by the second independent reviewer, issues emerging during ordinary revision, and supported source-pool issues absent from each condition's final primary and secondary output. Do not credit a stage for an unsupported formulation merely because another stage expressed a supported version of the same issue.

Source-reference and accounting checks do not establish scientific correctness. Invalid ratings remain unscored without another judge call, and make preferences provisional. Semantic clustering is a model judgment and requires inspection before interpreting gains or losses. A review comment can contain multiple requests, so coarse grouping can overstate retention. Supplementary unresolved hypothesis notes without an anchored original finding remain explicitly unscored.

Keep both workflows frozen across the two papers. After completion, audit failures and write results before making any further prompt changes. Do not infer superiority, saturation, or publication readiness from two papers or one same-family judge per paper.
